Three programmes, pre-registered, with every threshold fixed before the data was touched. This page is the record of what they found — read live from the registries on master, so it cannot quietly go stale the way its predecessor did — and, above that record, what the system is looking at today.

The programmes

Each carries a K budget: a fixed number of reads. Declaring a specification costs nothing; reading one spends a slot permanently, and anti-deferral means it can never be re-read — not even if a bug turns up afterwards. The budget is what stops a search from running until it finds a number it likes.

The reads

Every criterion below was written into the registry before the data existed. Nothing was chosen after seeing a result, and each specification records its own prior — the probability the operator signed, in advance, that it would pass.

What the record actually shows

The same failure appears in three separate reads, across two programmes and two unrelated mechanisms: the effect is absent from the first third of the sample.

Stability asks whether an edge runs through the whole window or sits concentrated in part of it. form4_cluster_buy_v2, value_ebit_ev_v1 and value_ebit_ev_v2 each returned a non-positive first third. An effect that is not there for part of the sample is not one to rely on for the next part — and that is more durable than any individual verdict.

The v25 pipeline that came before these — price-derived features, 280 large caps, a five-day horizon, daily rebalance — returned a walk-forward AUC of 0.4942 over roughly 160,000 observations, zero of 305 models with a positive deflated Sharpe, and White Reality Check and Hansen SPA p-values of 0.486 and 0.948. It was retired on 2026-09-29 and its code deleted on 2026-11-08.

What survives

The engineering, which was never the part that failed. A point-in-time universe and fundamentals panel built without look-ahead — the half most people get wrong and never find out. qt/, twelve tested modules. A Referee that counts reads and refuses a second one. And the pre-registration discipline that makes a negative answer worth trusting.

This page reads specifications.json and v29_specifications.json from master on every load. The dashboard it replaced was fed by a build step inside quant_runner.py; when that file was deleted the feed froze, and the page carried on showing a seventeen-position book the account had not held since September. A record that can quietly stop being true is worse than no record — so this one shows nothing rather than something stale.
