Confidence ⓘ
Confidence Score
The model's overall conviction in its signal, blending ML probability with Kelly sizing and regime adjustment. >70% = strong signal. 55–70% = moderate. <55% = weak/cautious.
—
ML Score ⓘ
Ensemble ML Score
The raw probability output averaged across three models: XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost. Higher agreement = more reliable signal. Divergence between models lowers confidence.
—
RSI ⓘ
Relative Strength Index
14-day momentum oscillator on a 0–100 scale.
>70 = Overbought (may pull back)
<30 = Oversold (potential bounce)
30–70 = Neutral momentum
—
Regime ⓘ
Market Regime (HMM)
Detected by a Hidden Markov Model trained on macro indicators.
BULL = trending up, full sizing
FLAT = range-bound, selective entries
BEAR = downtrend, reduced sizing & strict filters
—
Entry Price ⓘ
Price at Prediction
The stock price recorded at the time the model generated this signal. Used as the benchmark for calculating actual return when the prediction is scored 5 trading days later.
